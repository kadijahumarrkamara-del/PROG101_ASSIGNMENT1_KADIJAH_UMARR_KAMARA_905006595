# PROG101_Assignment1_Kadijah_Kamara_905006595
Clini_Queue is a digital system management system designed to improve patient flow and reduce waiting times in health care facilities
# PROJECT BACKGROUND
Many clinics in Sierra Leone still manage waiting lines by hand, and since the 2010 Free Health Care Initiative they serve more patients than ever. Paper lists lead to missed names, patients called out of turn and uncertain waits. The Clinic Queue Helper System is a simple front-desk tool that gives each patient a token, keeps a fair first-come, first-served queue, lets staff mark priority patients, and calls the next patient only when the room and health worker are ready.
# HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS
1. Staff open the main menu: Register patient, Call next patient, View queue, Exit.
2. To register, staff enter the patient's ID and name.
3. The system checks both are filled in and asks again if not.
4. The system issues a token (such as A015) and records the arrival time.
5. Staff say whether the patient is marked for priority.
6. Priority patients join the priority queue; everyone else joins the end of the normal queue.
7. The system shows the patient's token and queue position.
8. To call a patient, staff confirm the room and health worker are ready.
9. The system calls the next patient: priority first, then in order of arrival.
10. After the consultation, staff confirm it's finished and the patient is marked completed.
11. Staff can view the full queue at any time.
12. On exit, the system shows how many patients were served.

# SDG ALIGNMENT
The Clinic Queue Helper System supports Sustainable Development Goal 3, Good Health and Well-being, and in particular target 3.8 on access to quality essential health-care services for all. A fair, organised queue means patients know when they will be seen, priority cases are handled sooner, and staff spend less time managing the line and more time on care. The system is an administrative tool only: it does not diagnose patients or improve medical treatment by itself, but it helps clinics deliver their existing services in a more orderly and equitable way.
